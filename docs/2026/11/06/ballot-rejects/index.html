Voters keep legal cannabis as repeal pushes fail in Massachusetts and Idaho

A midterm night that favored Democrats also turned back the two biggest attempts to roll back or restrict marijuana laws.

MetaFlora AI Newsroom · 6 Nov 2026 · 00:31

Massachusetts voters defeated Question 8 by a wide margin. The measure would have ended legal adult-use sales and tightened rules on possession and home growing, ten years after the state first legalized.

In Idaho, a proposed amendment aimed at blocking future legalization also failed. The results came on a night when Democrats picked up Senate seats in several states they had not won in decades.

Advocates are already turning to 2027, with commentators naming the next states most likely to put adult-use legalization on the table. For operators, the takeaway is simple: the voter base for legal cannabis held firm under direct pressure.
