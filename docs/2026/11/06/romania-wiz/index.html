Romanian court cancels Wiz Khalifa's prison sentence over festival joint

The nine-month sentence from a 2024 festival appearance has been overturned along with its enforcement warrant.

MetaFlora AI Newsroom · 6 Nov 2026 · 00:35

A court in Romania has overturned the nine-month prison sentence handed to the rapper for smoking cannabis on stage at a 2024 music festival. The ruling cancels both the conviction and the warrant to enforce it.

Romania remains one of Europe's stricter jurisdictions on personal use. The reversal is a rare high-profile softening, and a signal worth watching for anyone building cannabis or events business in the region.
